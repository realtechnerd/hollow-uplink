      Hollow Uplink

      For thirty years the Directorate has told the Hollow that the surface air kills in minutes. Nobody has been up to check. The machines left behind are still running, and this is what they measure.

      1. The city starts dark. Scout robots map it with LiDAR as they explore. Every tile is a signed scan, and it fades as the scan gets old.
      2. Surface report, top left: the verifier's plain-language answer for each zone (outposts, domes, suits or not safe), and any claim it has sent a machine to check.
      3. Map layers, right edge: one measurement at a time, like radiation, air quality or CO₂, from trusted machines only. Grey means nobody has measured there yet.
      4. Click any machine for its readings and signature check. Forged, tampered or replayed messages are rejected and show in the feed, bottom left.

      The machines are simulated. The protocols (MQTT, Modbus TCP), translation, signing and verification are real code.
