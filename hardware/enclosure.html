BX-1 sensor box

A two-part printed enclosure (open base plus lift-off lid) for the Uno field station's sensors. Front, top and right views in third-angle projection, a section through the lid, and an exploded isometric. All dimensions in millimetres. The origin is the outer front-left-bottom corner of the base: X runs across the width, Y runs front to back, Z runs up. The front is the long wall that faces the camera. The boards are stuck on with foam tape or hot glue, so there are no screw holes. Teal outlines are engraved into the walls to show where each board goes, sized to the measured boards plus about 1 mm all round.

On a narrow screen, scroll the sheet sideways. Red tags match the feature schedule; teal outlines match the board table.

Notes

  • The lid lifts straight off and drops back on. The lip keeps it lined up; nothing latches or hinges.
  • Mount the boards with double-sided foam tape, hot glue on the board edges, or Blu-Tack. Keep glue off the MQ-2's metal can.
  • Crash sensor: tape it in loosely inside its outline, put the lid on and lift it off a few times. Move it until hatch_open reads 0 with the lid on and 1 with it off, then press it down firmly. The free lever should stand about 2 to 4 mm above Z = 88.
  • Engrave the back-wall and right-wall outlines on the inside faces. Seen from inside the back wall, the left wall is on your left, so X reads normally.
  • The MQ-2 heater gets warm. Print in PETG or ASA rather than PLA if you can.
  • Print the base open side up and the lid upside down (top face on the bed). The Ø16 cable hole prints cleaner as a teardrop (point up) or with a little support.
  • No screws are needed.
